My Approach

The right system is the one that works.

I don't believe in adding complexity just to make a process look more sophisticated.

I look at how the business actually operates, who is involved, what information needs to move, and what each person needs in order to do their part well.

From there, I work within existing systems or create practical workflows where something new is needed.

Good systems should make the work easier for the people using them, not make the people work harder to use the system.
