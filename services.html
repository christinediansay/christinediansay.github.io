My Approach

The right system is the one that works.

I don't start with a tool and try to fit the business around it. I look at how the work actually operates, who is involved, what information needs to move, and what each person needs to do their part.

Sometimes the answer is working within an existing system. Sometimes it means creating a practical workflow around it. The goal is not to make things more technical. It's to make the work easier to manage.

Good systems should make the work easier for the people using them, not make the people work harder to use the system.
