Media & Publishing | United States

End-to-End Magazine Production & Team Coordination

Managing a multi-stage publishing process involving contributors, editors, designers, web, social media, and distribution, while keeping the client out of the day-to-day technical complexity.

The Challenge

Magazine production involved multiple people, deadlines, files, approvals, and platforms. Contributors needed to submit materials, editors needed access to content, designers needed clear production status, and the client needed visibility without having to manage the technical workflow herself.

My Approach

I built a practical production workflow around the way the team actually worked. The goal was not to introduce more technology, but to create a clear structure for moving information, responsibilities, and deliverables from one stage to the next.

What I Managed

Contributor Outreach Submission Management Google Drive Organization Production Tracking Editorial Coordination Trello Production Workflow Design Team Coordination Quality Control Social Media Production Website Updates Digital Distribution Print-on-Demand Contributor Communication

How the Workflow Worked

01 · Intake Contributor outreach and submission collection
02 · Organize Files, author information, photos, and notes organized by issue
03 · Track High-level issue tracker maintained for status and accountability
04 · Edit Articles coordinated with the editorial team
05 · Produce Article-level Trello cards used to coordinate layout and design
06 · Publish Final checks, distribution, website updates, and contributor communication

What This Demonstrates

This project required more than keeping a task list. It required understanding how different parts of production depended on one another, anticipating what each team member needed next, checking details before they reached the client, and keeping the entire process moving toward publication.
